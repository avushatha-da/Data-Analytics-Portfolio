# 📊 HR Employee Attrition Analysis

## Project Overview

This project analyzes employee attrition to identify workforce patterns and potential factors associated with employee turnover.

The project follows an end-to-end data analytics workflow, starting with data preparation and cleaning, followed by SQL-based analysis and validation, and finally the development of an interactive Power BI dashboard.

The analysis focuses on employee demographics, job characteristics, employee experience, career progression, compensation, and other workforce factors that may be associated with employee attrition.

---

## 🎯 Business Problem

Employee attrition can create challenges for organizations through recruitment costs, loss of experienced employees, workforce instability, and increased hiring and training requirements.

The purpose of this project is to analyze employee data and identify patterns in employee turnover that can help HR teams better understand potential attrition drivers and support data-driven retention planning.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Understand the overall employee attrition situation.
- Identify departments and job roles with higher employee exits.
- Analyze employee satisfaction and workplace experience.
- Examine career progression and employee tenure.
- Analyze demographic and workforce characteristics.
- Evaluate compensation and benefits-related factors.
- Identify important patterns associated with employee attrition.
- Develop business-oriented insights.
- Build an interactive HR analytics dashboard.
